About

I'm a Television, Radio, and Film student at Syracuse University's Newhouse School with a minor in Animation and VFX. I'm a Newhouse Leadership Scholar and a Renee Crown Honors student.

In spring 2026 I studied at Waseda University in Tokyo, where I researched and filmed a cultural film project on community-based filmmaking.

I edit in Premiere Pro and After Effects, design in Photoshop and InDesign, and animate in Maya, Blender, and Toon Boom Harmony.
